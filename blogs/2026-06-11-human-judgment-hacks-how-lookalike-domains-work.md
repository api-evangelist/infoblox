Lookalike attack success does not rely on exploiting software flaws or compromised infrastructure. It plays on human tendencies to read what we expect, accept what we see and mentally autocorrect what we read. By designing domain names that resemble common services, roles or contexts, attackers exploit how people recognize and trust familiar patterns.
